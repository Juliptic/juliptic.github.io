We review how to construct an L-function from a representation $$ \rho\colon Gal(\bar{K}/K) \to GL(V) $$

Assume that the representation factors through some $$Gal(\bar{K}/K)\to Gal(L/K)\to GL(V)$$, then we can define 
$$L(s,\rho)=\prod_{\mathfrak{p}\subset \mathcal{O}_K}det(1-N(\mathfrak{p})^{-s}\rho(Frob_{\mathfrak{p}}) | V^{I_{\mathfrak{p}}} )^{-1}$$. 

We will prove that this definition is well defined. First we note that at all but finitely many primes, $$I_{\mathfrak{p}}=\{1\}$$ since all but finitely many primes are unramified.
